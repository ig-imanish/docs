Betterumami is a privacy-first web analytics platform that gives you the complete picture of your website — traffic, campaigns, behavior, conversions, and revenue — all in one place. Built on the open-source Umami engine and enhanced with a fully managed cloud experience, Betterumami tracks what matters without ever using cookies or collecting personal data. You get instant, accurate insights from a lightweight script that has zero performance impact on your site.

## Get started in three steps

You can have Betterumami collecting data on your website in under five minutes. Follow the steps below to set up your account and install the tracking script.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [betterumami.com/register](https://www.betterumami.com/register) and sign up with your email or Google account. Verify your email address when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your website">
    In the Betterumami dashboard, click **Websites** in the sidebar, then click **Add website**. Enter a name and your domain, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the tracking script">
    Click **Edit** on your website, copy the tracking code snippet, and paste it into the `<head>` section of every page you want to track. Data will start appearing in your dashboard immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
