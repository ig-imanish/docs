## Recommended path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install tracking">
    Complete the [quickstart](/docs/quickstart) and confirm that Datasite receives activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add meaningful data">
    Track product events, identify signed-in visitors when appropriate, and enable only the additional collection you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build reports">
    Create filters, segments, goals, funnels, and experiments after the necessary data starts arriving.
  </Step>
</Steps>
