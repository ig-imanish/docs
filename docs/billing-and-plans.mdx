Datasite applies each plan to a workspace. Your plan determines how many websites and members the workspace can have, its monthly event allowance, and the analytics features available to it.

## Compare plans

| Allowance | Free | Starter | Pro | Ultra |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Websites | 1 | 3 | 10 | 50 |
| Members | 1 | 2 | 5 | 20 |
| Events per month | 100,000 | 250,000 | 1,000,000 | 10,000,000 |
| Funnels | 1 | 3 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Goals | 3 | 10 | 50 | Unlimited |
| Experiments | 1 | 3 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Custom event names | 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Data retention | 6 months | 12 months | 24 months | 60 months |

<Note>
  Prices, billing intervals, and available currencies are shown on the pricing page and during checkout.
</Note>

## Upgrade a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the workspace">
    Switch to the workspace you want to upgrade. Plans and allowances apply to the selected workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open billing">
    Open **Billing** or **Plans** from the workspace settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select the plan you want. Choose monthly or yearly billing when those options are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    Enter the required details in the secure checkout and confirm the subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the active plan">
    Return to Datasite and verify that the workspace shows the correct active plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage billing

Open the customer portal from the **Billing** page. Depending on the options available for your subscription, you can manage:

- Your payment method
- Your subscription
- Invoices from the payment provider

The checkout or customer portal shows when a plan change takes effect and any terms that apply to the change.

## Monitor usage

Open the **Usage** page to review event consumption and resource usage for the current workspace. Monthly event allowances reset according to the workspace's billing or usage period.

When a workspace reaches a plan limit, Datasite may prevent you from creating additional resources or accepting usage above the allowance. Review the Usage page before adding websites, members, funnels, goals, experiments, or custom event names.
