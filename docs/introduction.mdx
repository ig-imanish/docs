Betterumami is a fully managed, privacy-first web analytics platform built on the open-source [Umami](https://umami.is) engine. It brings together traffic analysis, event tracking, conversion funnels, revenue attribution, and AI-powered querying in a single lightweight tool — all without collecting any personal data, setting cookies, or requiring a cookie consent banner. Whether you run a personal blog, a SaaS product, or an e-commerce store, Betterumami gives you the complete picture of your audience while respecting their privacy by default.

## What makes Betterumami different

Unlike traditional analytics platforms that rely on cookie-based tracking and collect personally identifiable information, Betterumami is designed around privacy from the ground up. Nothing personally identifiable is stored. You stay compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR without any extra configuration. And because the tracking script is tiny and asynchronous, it adds no measurable impact to your page load times.

## How it works

Betterumami works by placing a small JavaScript snippet in the `<head>` of your website. That script sends anonymized event data — pageviews, clicks, and custom events — to the Betterumami cloud. No cookies are written to the browser, and no IP addresses or user agents are stored in identifiable form. The data is processed and made available in your dashboard in real time.

Because the tracking is cookieless and collects no personal data, most websites can use Betterumami without displaying a cookie consent banner. You should always consult your own legal counsel to confirm compliance for your specific situation.
