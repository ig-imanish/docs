Betterumami gives you a privacy-first analytics platform you can be up and running with in minutes. This guide walks you through every step of account management — from registering and verifying your email, to upgrading your plan and controlling which emails land in your inbox.

## Create an account

Sign up for free at [betterumami.com/register](https://betterumami.com/register). You can register with an email address and password, or use **Continue with Google** to sign up instantly with your Google account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Enter your email address and choose a strong password, then click **Sign up**. If you prefer, click **Continue with Google** to skip the form entirely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Betterumami. Open it and enter the **6-digit code** on the verification screen. The code expires after a short window, so complete this step promptly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete account setup">
    After verification you land on the setup page. You must complete two steps before your account is fully active:

    - **Verify email address** — confirms your identity and enables all email features.
    - **Select data region** — choose where your analytics data is stored (Europe or United States). This choice affects data residency and the API region you use later.

    Once both steps are checked off, click **Continue** to enter your dashboard. You can also press **Skip setup** at the bottom of the screen to proceed immediately and return to settings later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your data region cannot be changed after setup. Choose carefully based on where your visitors and your legal obligations are located.
</Note>

## Log in

Visit [betterumami.com](https://betterumami.com) and click **Log in**. Enter your email and password, then click **Log in** to access your dashboard.

### Reset a forgotten password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login page">
    Go to [betterumami.com](https://betterumami.com) and click **Log in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password?">
    Below the password field, click the **Forgot password?** link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type the email address associated with your account and click **Send reset email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the link in your email">
    Open the password-reset email and click the link inside. You will be taken to a screen where you can set a new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Change your password

If you are already logged in and want to update your password, navigate to your **Account** page (click the profile icon in the side navigation) and click the **Change password** button.

## Subscription and billing

Betterumami uses **Stripe** as its payment processor. All plans include a **14-day free trial** — you will not be charged if you cancel within that window.

### Upgrade to Pro

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the profile icon in the side navigation bar and select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Billing">
    In the Settings menu, click **Billing**. You will see the available plans listed here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Upgrade to Pro">
    Click the **Upgrade to Pro** button. You will be redirected to the Stripe checkout portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    Enter your payment details in Stripe and confirm your subscription. After checkout you are returned to the Billing page, which now shows your active plan and next billing date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you need an **Enterprise** plan with higher data limits or custom terms, click the **Contact Us** button on the Billing page or email [sales@betterumami.com](mailto:sales@betterumami.com).
</Tip>

### Manage your plan

To update or cancel your subscription at any time, go to **Settings → Billing** and click **Manage plan**. This opens the Stripe billing portal where you can:

- Cancel your Pro plan
- Update your payment method
- Update billing contact information
- Review your invoice history
- Download invoices and receipts

<Warning>
  Cancelling your plan takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. Pro features (Teams, Email Reports, API keys, etc.) remain active until that date.
</Warning>

## Notification preferences

Betterumami sends occasional emails about new product features and platform updates. You can opt out of any of these at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the profile icon in the side navigation and select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Notifications">
    Click **Notifications** in the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle your preferences">
    Use the toggles to turn individual email notification types on or off. Changes are saved automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Transactional emails — such as email verification codes and password-reset links — are always sent regardless of your notification preferences.
</Note>
