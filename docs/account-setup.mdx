This guide explains how to create and verify your Datasite account, manage your password, and set email preferences.

## Create an account

Create an account with your email address and password. Depending on your deployment, you may also be able to sign in with Google or GitHub.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Enter your email address and password, then click **Sign up**. To use Google, click **Continue with Google**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Datasite. Enter the **6-digit code** on the verification screen before it expires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete account setup">
    After verification, the setup page asks you to complete two steps:

    - **Verify email address** to confirm your address.
    - **Select data region** to choose where Datasite stores your analytics data: Europe or the United States. This choice determines the API region you use.

    Complete both steps and click **Continue** to open your dashboard. To proceed without completing setup, click **Skip setup** and return to settings later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You cannot change your data region after setup. Choose the region that meets your data residency requirements.
</Note>

## Log in

To sign in, open Datasite, click **Log in**, and enter your email and password.

### Reset a forgotten password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login page">
    Open Datasite and click **Log in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Forgot password">
    Select **Forgot password?** below the password field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Enter the email address associated with your account and select **Send reset email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the link in your email">
    Open the password-reset email and follow its link to set a new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Change your password

To change your password while signed in, open **Account** from the profile menu and select **Change password**.

## Notification preferences

Datasite sends occasional emails about product features and platform updates. You can change your preferences at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the profile icon in the side navigation and select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Notifications">
    Click **Notifications** in the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle your preferences">
    Use the toggles to turn individual email notification types on or off. Changes are saved automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Transactional emails, including verification codes and password-reset links, are sent regardless of your notification preferences.
</Note>
