Email Reports give you a regular snapshot of how your website is performing, delivered straight to your inbox — or to anyone else who needs to stay informed. Instead of logging into the dashboard every day, you receive a neatly formatted summary at a predictable time each morning. Email Reports are available on the **Pro plan** and above.

## Delivery schedule

Reports are sent once per day at 7 AM in your account's data region timezone:

| Region | Delivery time |
|---|---|
| **Europe** | 7:00 AM CET / CEST |
| **United States** | 7:00 AM PST / PDT |

Your data region is set during account setup. If you are unsure which region your account uses, check **Settings → Billing** where your region is displayed.

---

## Available report types

### Website Summary

The **Website Summary** report delivers a concise overview of your site's recent performance. Each email includes:

- **Top stats** — total pageviews, unique visitors, visits, and bounce rate for the reporting period.
- **Top 10 pages** — the most visited pages ranked by pageview count.
- **Top referrers** — the external sources sending the most traffic to your site.

<Tip>
  Website Summary reports are ideal for sharing with stakeholders who want a regular read on traffic without needing direct dashboard access.
</Tip>

## Create an email report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the profile icon in the side navigation bar and select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Email reports">
    Click **Email reports** in the Settings menu. You will see a list of any existing reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create report">
    Click the **Create report** button to open the creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the report details">
    Complete the form fields:

    - **Report type** — select **Website Summary** (currently the only available type).
    - **Website** — choose the website you want to report on.
    - **Recipients** — enter one or more email addresses that should receive the report.
    - **Frequency** — select how often to send the report (daily or weekly, depending on available options).

    Click **Save** when you are done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Recipients do not need a Betterumami account to receive email reports. You can send summaries to clients or stakeholders who are not registered users.
</Note>

## Edit or delete an email report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Email reports">
    Navigate to **Settings → Email reports** to see your list of active reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a report">
    Click the **Edit** button next to the report you want to change. Update any fields in the form and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete a report">
    Click the **Delete** button next to the report you want to remove. Confirm the deletion when prompted. The report stops sending immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a report is permanent. If you need to pause delivery temporarily, consider editing the recipient list instead of deleting the report entirely.
</Warning>
