Datasite can publish selected analytics through share URLs, combine components into boards, track redirect links, and count pixel requests. Treat every public share URL as accessible to anyone who receives it.

## Public sharing

Sharing creates a public URL for a selected website, board, tracked link, or pixel. Do not share private filters or sensitive analytics. Where available, sharing settings can include a display name, logo, or domain for white-label presentation. Remove the share when public access is no longer needed.

## Boards

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a board">
    Open **Boards**, create a board, and enter its name and description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design the view">
    Open the designer, then add and arrange supported components.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the board">
    Save the board. You can edit, clone, view, and share it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tracked links

A tracked link has a name, destination URL, and unique short slug. Requests to the short URL record analytics and redirect to the destination. Use the link detail page for charts and sharing. Changing or deleting a link affects future redirects. Existing links use globally unique slugs.

## Tracking pixels

A tracking pixel has a name and unique slug. Place its public pixel URL where a one-pixel request is appropriate, such as an HTML email system that permits remote images. Pixel requests appear in its analytics page.

<Note>
  Email clients, privacy proxies, image caching, and blocked remote images make open counts approximate. A pixel request does not prove that a person read a message.
</Note>
