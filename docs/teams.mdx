Teams let you share your Betterumami analytics with colleagues, clients, or contractors without giving anyone access to your personal account. Every team member gets a role that precisely controls what they can see and do, keeping your data organized and your account secure. Teams are available on the **Pro plan** and above.

## Roles and permissions

Each member of a team is assigned one of four roles. Choose the role that matches the level of trust and responsibility you want to grant.

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| **Team Owner** | Full access — create, edit, delete the team; manage members; manage websites. |
| **Team Manager** | All permissions except deleting the team. |
| **Team Member** | All permissions except deleting or updating the team settings. |
| **View Only** | Can view analytics dashboards only — no administrative actions. |

<Note>
  Only the **Team Owner** can edit or remove other team members.
</Note>

## Create a team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the profile icon in the side navigation bar and select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Teams">
    Click **Teams** in the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create team">
    Click the **Create team** button to open the creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the team details">
    Enter a name for your team and any other required fields, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You are automatically added to the new team as **Team Owner**.

## Share your team access code

Every team has a unique access code that lets others join without a direct invitation. Share this code with anyone you want to add quickly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your team">
    Navigate to **Settings → Teams** and click on the team name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the access code">
    The first panel on the team page shows the team's access code. Copy it and send it to the people you want to invite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Regenerate if needed">
    If the code has been shared too broadly, click **Regenerate**, then save. The old code is immediately invalidated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Anyone with the access code can join your team. Regenerate it if you suspect it has been shared with unintended recipients.
</Warning>

## Join a team

If a team owner has given you an access code, you can join their team directly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Teams">
    Navigate to **Settings → Teams**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Join team">
    Click the **Join team** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the access code">
    Paste or type the access code provided by the team owner, then click **Join**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite a member by email

For a more controlled onboarding experience, invite people by email directly from the team page. They will receive an email with a link and instructions to join.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your team">
    Go to **Settings → Teams** and click on the team name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Members tab">
    Click the **Members** tab inside the team view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite user">
    Click **Invite user** to open the invitation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the invitation details">
    Enter the recipient's email address and select the role you want to assign them, then click **Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a website to a team

Websites added to a team are visible to all team members according to their role.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your team">
    Go to **Settings → Teams** and click on the team name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Websites tab">
    Click the **Websites** tab inside the team view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add website">
    Click **Add website** and fill in the website details (name, domain, etc.), then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Transfer a website between your account and a team

You can move a website you own into a team, or pull a website from a team back to your personal account — as long as you are the Team Owner.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Website settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Websites** and click the **Edit** button for the website you want to move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Transfer section">
    Scroll to the **Transfer website** section at the bottom of the edit form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Transfer">
    Click the **Transfer** button, choose the destination (your account or a team you own), and confirm the transfer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can only transfer websites to teams where you hold the **Team Owner** role.
</Note>

## Edit or remove a team member

Only the **Team Owner** can change a member's role or remove them from the team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your team's Members list">
    Go to **Settings → Teams**, click the team name, then click the **Members** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit or remove">
    - Click **Edit** next to a member to change their role, then save.
    - Click **Delete** next to a member to remove them from the team immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
