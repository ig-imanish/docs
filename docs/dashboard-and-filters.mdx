Datasite dashboards help you review traffic for a selected period, compare it with a previous period, and narrow compatible charts and tables to the audience or activity you need.

## Overview metrics

- **Page views:** Recorded page-view events.
- **Visitors:** Distinct visitors in the selected period.
- **Visits:** Visit or session activity in the period.
- **Bounce rate:** Visits that did not continue beyond the bounce criteria used by the report.
- **Visit duration:** Accumulated or average session time shown by the selected card or report.

Use the date control to select a preset or custom period. Comparison mode shows the corresponding previous period where supported.

## Breakdowns

Open a metric or breakdown to group traffic by page, referrer, channel, country, region, city, browser, operating system, device, screen, language, hostname, UTM value, tag, or another available dimension.

## Filters and segments

Filters narrow every compatible chart and table. Common filters include page path, referrer, event, country, browser, device, campaign, and custom property. Multiple filters may require all selected conditions.

Save a commonly used filter set as a segment. Segments belong to one website and can be reopened from that website's **Segments** page.

## Annotations

Annotations add a dated note to charts, such as a release or campaign launch. An annotation can cover an entire day or a specific timestamp. Notes accept up to 500 characters.

## Missing or surprising totals

Totals can differ when filters, timezones, imported aggregate data, retention, ignored traffic, or bot and traffic rules differ. A date comparison uses its own matching period. It is not an all-time total.
