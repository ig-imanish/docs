# Email reports

Copy page

Send scheduled email reports to anyone. Email Reports is available starting at the **Pro** plan.

Email reports are sent out at the following times for your account region.

- **Europe:** 7AM CET/CEST
- **United States:** 7AM PST/PDT

## Available reports[#](https://docs.umami.is/docs/cloud/email-reports#available-reports)

- **Website Summary:** Your website's top stats including top ten pages and referrers.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/cloud/email-report-website-summary.png)

## Create an email report[#](https://docs.umami.is/docs/cloud/email-reports#create-an-email-report)

Log into Umami and click on the side nav profile button then click **Settings**.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/cloud/navbar-settings.png)

Navigate to **Email reports** and click on the **Create report** button.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/cloud/email-report-create.png)

Fill out the form details and click the **Save** button.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/cloud/email-report-create-form.png)

## Edit or Delete an email report[#](https://docs.umami.is/docs/cloud/email-reports#edit-or-delete-an-email-report)

Navigate to **Email reports** and click on the **Edit** button or **Delete** button next to your email report.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/cloud/email-report-table.png)
