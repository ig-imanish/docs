# Login

Copy page

You can log into your account at [https://cloud.umami.is/login](https://cloud.umami.is/login?ref=umami-cloud-docs).

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/cloud/login-form.png)

If you forget your password click the **Forgot password?** button and enter your email to receive an email to reset your password.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/cloud/login-forgot-password.png)

You can change your password by navigating to **Account** and click the **Change password** button.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/cloud/account-manage.png)
