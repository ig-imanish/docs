# Segments

Copy page

_Available since v3.0.0_

Segments let you save commonly used filters in Umami, so you can quickly reapply them without setting criteria each time. This makes it easy to analyze specific groups of users and track their behavior consistently over time. It speeds up analysis and ensures your insights remain focused and repeatable.

## Add a segment[#](https://docs.umami.is/docs/segments#add-a-segment)

There are two methods to add a segment.

**Method 1:** Click on **Segments** from the side nav then click on the **Add segment** button.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/docs/segment-add.png)

Choose your filters and click **Save**.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/docs/segment-filter.png)

**Method 2:** From the website **Overview** page select your filters then click **Save as segment**.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/docs/segment-save.png)

## Applying a segment[#](https://docs.umami.is/docs/segments#applying-a-segment)

Add a filter and switch to the **Segments** tab. Click on a segment and click on the **Apply** button.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/docs/segment-apply.png)

## Edit or Delete a segment[#](https://docs.umami.is/docs/segments#edit-or-delete-a-segment)

From the **Segments** screen click on the **Edit** button to update a segment or click on the **Delete** button to delete a segment.

![image](https://docs.umami.is/images/docs/segment-table.png)
