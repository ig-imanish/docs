## Protect your audience's privacy

Umami collects no personal data, uses no cookies, and never identifies individual visitors. Whether your audience includes children, patients, students, or vulnerable communities, their privacy is protected by default — supporting your GDPR, CCPA, and COPPA compliance.

![Protect your audience's privacy](https://umami.is/images/screenshot-dashboard.jpg)

## Measure the impact of your campaigns

Track donations, volunteer signups, petition signatures, and event registrations with custom events. See which campaigns drive the most engagement and use UTM tracking to understand which outreach channels work best.

![Measure the impact of your campaigns](https://umami.is/images/feature-events.png)

## Report to stakeholders with clear data

Generate share links so board members, grant funders, and partners can view your analytics without needing an account. Schedule email reports with traffic summaries and key metrics to keep stakeholders informed automatically.

![Report to stakeholders with clear data](https://umami.is/images/feature-stats.png)

## Understand your supporters

See where your visitors come from geographically, which devices they use, and how they find your site. Use these insights to tailor your content, plan regional campaigns, and allocate resources where they'll have the most impact.

![Understand your supporters](https://umami.is/images/screenshot-location.jpg)

## Optimize your donation funnel

Build funnels to measure each step from awareness to action. See where potential donors or volunteers drop off, and use user journeys to understand the paths people take through your site before they convert.

![Optimize your donation funnel](https://umami.is/images/feature-funnels.png)
