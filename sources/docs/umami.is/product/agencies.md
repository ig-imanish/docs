## Build custom reports for each client

Create tailored reports that highlight the metrics each client cares about. Combine traffic data, conversion events, UTM performance, and revenue into focused views that make monthly reporting effortless.

## Share analytics without sharing logins

Generate unique share links so clients can view their analytics in any browser — no login required. Or invite clients as team members with scoped permissions so they only see their own data.
