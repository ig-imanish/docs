# Introduction

Copy page

Umami is a web analytics platform that brings traffic, campaigns, visitor behavior, conversions, and revenue together in one place. It gives you a complete picture of how people find and use your website, from their first visit to the outcomes that matter to your business.

These docs cover **Umami v3**. For the previous version, see the [v2 documentation](https://v2.umami.is).

![Umami analytics dashboard](https://docs.umami.is/images/docs/screenshot.png)

## Understand your website from end to end[#](https://docs.umami.is/docs#understand-your-website-from-end-to-end)

- **Measure acquisition** — See pageviews, visitors, referrers, locations, devices, and real-time activity. Track [UTM campaigns](https://docs.umami.is/docs/utm), [links](https://docs.umami.is/docs/links), and [pixels](https://docs.umami.is/docs/pixels) to understand which channels bring people to your site.
- **Analyze behavior** — Go beyond pageviews with [custom events](https://docs.umami.is/docs/track-events), [journeys](https://docs.umami.is/docs/journey), [session replays](https://docs.umami.is/docs/replays), and [heatmaps](https://docs.umami.is/docs/heatmaps). Use [segments](https://docs.umami.is/docs/segments), [cohorts](https://docs.umami.is/docs/cohorts), and [retention](https://docs.umami.is/docs/retention) to examine the audiences and patterns behind your metrics.
- **Improve conversion** — Define the outcomes you care about with [goals](https://docs.umami.is/docs/goals), build [funnels](https://docs.umami.is/docs/funnel) to find drop-off points, and use [attribution](https://docs.umami.is/docs/attribution) to connect conversions to the marketing that influenced them.
- **Connect traffic to revenue** — Attach values to events and use the [revenue report](https://docs.umami.is/docs/revenue) to see which pages, campaigns, and visitor segments generate the most value.
- **Share and extend your analytics** — Create focused dashboards with [boards](https://docs.umami.is/docs/using-boards), collaborate through [teams](https://docs.umami.is/docs/using-teams), share website statistics, or use the [API](https://docs.umami.is/docs/api) to build your own reports and integrations.

## Privacy without sacrificing insight[#](https://docs.umami.is/docs#privacy-without-sacrificing-insight)

Umami provides these capabilities without cookies, cross-site tracking, or the automatic collection of personal data. You can use the managed cloud service or self-host Umami on your own infrastructure, keeping control over where your analytics data lives.

## Get started[#](https://docs.umami.is/docs#get-started)

Choose the setup that fits your team:

[Umami Cloud\\ \\ Managed hosting, updates, backups, and scaling.\\ \\ Create a free account](https://cloud.umami.is/signup?ref=umami-docs-introduction) [Self-hosted\\ \\ Run the open-source platform on your own infrastructure.\\ \\ View installation guide](https://docs.umami.is/docs/install)

You can also use one of the [deployment guides](https://docs.umami.is/docs/guides/hosting) to run Umami on a managed hosting platform.

Once Umami is running:

1. [Add your website](https://docs.umami.is/docs/add-a-website).
2. [Install the tracking script](https://docs.umami.is/docs/collect-data).
3. Open your website dashboard and start exploring your data.
