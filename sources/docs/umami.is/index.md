## Where your traffic actually comes from

Channels, referrers, and campaigns attributed end to end, so you know what's working before you spend more on it.

### All your key metrics on one dashboard

Your key metrics — pageviews, visitors, bounce rate, and average visit time — all on one dashboard. Pick any date range, filter by country or device, and get an answer in seconds.

![All your key metrics on one dashboard](https://umami.is/images/screenshot-dashboard.jpg)

### Locations, devices, and referrers at a glance

See exactly where your audience is located, what devices and browsers they use, and which sites are sending you traffic. Understand your audience without collecting any personal data.

![Locations, devices, and referrers at a glance](https://umami.is/images/screenshot-location.jpg)

### Measure whether your campaigns are working

Compare any two time periods side by side to spot trends instantly. Track UTM parameters automatically, see which channels drive the most conversions with attribution, and measure whether your latest marketing push is actually moving the needle.

![Measure whether your campaigns are working](https://umami.is/images/screenshot-compare.jpg)

## What people do once they arrive

Events, user journeys, heatmaps, and session replay show how visitors move through your site — and exactly where they get stuck.

### Track the actions that matter

Go beyond pageviews. Track button clicks, form submissions, signups, downloads — any action that matters to your business. Define the events you care about, then watch session replays to see exactly what happened.

![Track the actions that matter](https://umami.is/images/screenshot-events.jpg)

## Where you lose people before they buy

Funnels and goals show how far visitors get toward the outcomes you care about — and which step is costing you the most.

### See where visitors drop off, and why

Build a funnel from any sequence of pages and events and see how many people make it through each step. Trace the exact path visitors take, then watch session replays to see what happened — where they got confused, what they clicked, and why they left.

![See where visitors drop off, and why](https://umami.is/images/screenshot-user-journey.jpg)

## What your traffic is actually worth

Revenue tracking connects every visit, channel, and campaign to the money it brought in.

### Connect your traffic to actual revenue

Tie events to dollar amounts and track revenue directly inside Umami. See which pages, campaigns, and user segments are driving the most value for your business.
