## Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Umami and Plausible?

Both are privacy-first, open-source analytics tools. The main difference is depth: Umami includes session replay, heatmaps, user journeys, cohorts, retention, and custom dashboards, while Plausible focuses on a single, simple stats page. Umami also uses the permissive MIT license, versus Plausible’s AGPL.

Is Umami cheaper than Plausible?

Umami Cloud has a free tier; Plausible’s cloud starts at $9/month with no free plan. Self-hosting is free for both, though Plausible’s self-hosted Community Edition trails its cloud product.

Does Plausible have session replay or heatmaps?

No. Plausible does not offer session replay or heatmaps. Umami offers both — on Umami Cloud Business plans and free when self-hosting — along with user journeys and custom reports, while remaining cookie-free and GDPR compliant.

Why does the MIT vs. AGPL license difference matter?

The MIT license lets you use, modify, and embed Umami in commercial products without restriction. The AGPL requires you to open source any modifications if you offer the software over a network, which can be a problem for commercial use.
