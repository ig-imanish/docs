## Real analytics, not an afterthought

Pendo is a product experience platform built for in-app guides, onboarding flows, and feedback surveys. Its analytics capabilities are secondary — even Pendo's own users describe its reporting as limited and inflexible. Umami is an analytics platform first: real-time dashboards, custom reports, funnels, journeys, session replay, and revenue tracking. You get the complete picture — from acquisition to conversion — instead of a bolted-on analytics tab.

## Up and running in minutes

Pendo requires SDK integration, tagging features within your app, and significant configuration to start collecting useful data. Umami works with a single script tag — add one line of code and you're immediately tracking pageviews, referrers, devices, and locations. No feature tagging, no SDK setup, and no onboarding process to get value from your analytics.

## Privacy as a foundation

Pendo identifies and tracks individual users, building detailed profiles of their in-app behavior. Umami takes the opposite approach — no cookies, no personal data collection, and no individual user tracking. You get aggregate insights about your website visitors while fully respecting their privacy and maintaining compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other regulations out of the box.

## Transparent pricing, no year-two surprises

Pendo uses enterprise pricing that requires contacting sales, and users frequently report unexpected price increases after their first year. Umami offers transparent, affordable pricing starting with a free tier. Self-hosting the open-source platform is always free, and paid plans are straightforward — the price you see today is the price you pay tomorrow.

## Frequently asked questions

Is Umami a good alternative to Pendo?

For analytics, yes. Umami delivers real website analytics — events, funnels, cohorts, session replay, heatmaps, and revenue tracking — rather than analytics bolted onto a product adoption suite. If you specifically need in-app guides or NPS surveys, those remain Pendo strengths.

Does Umami have session replay?

Yes. Session replay and heatmaps are built into Umami — available on Umami Cloud Business plans and free when self-hosting — alongside funnels, user journeys, and custom dashboards, all while staying cookie-free and GDPR compliant by default.

How does Umami pricing compare to Pendo?

Pendo uses custom enterprise pricing — you have to talk to sales, and costs often jump in year two. Umami Cloud is free to start with transparent paid plans from $20/month, and self-hosting is completely free.

How long does Umami take to set up?

Minutes. Umami is a single ~2KB script tag with no SDK or configuration project. Pendo typically requires SDK installation, tagging, and a longer implementation before you see value.
