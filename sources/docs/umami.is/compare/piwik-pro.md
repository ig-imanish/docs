## The free plan that disappeared

Piwik PRO discontinued its free Core plan — existing users were required to upgrade or migrate by early 2026, and new signups get only a 30-day trial before paying from €35/month. Umami's commitment runs the other way: the MIT-licensed software is free to self-host forever, and Umami Cloud keeps a genuinely free tier.

## Open source versus enterprise suite

Despite sharing ancestry with Matomo, Piwik PRO is closed-source, and true self-hosting is reserved for its top Enterprise tier. Umami is fully open source: inspect the code, run it on your own infrastructure with Docker and PostgreSQL, and keep complete control of your data without an enterprise contract.

## Analytics without the implementation project

Piwik PRO is a four-module enterprise suite — analytics, tag manager, consent manager, and CDP — with the onboarding, learning curve, and per-tier caps that implies. Umami delivers the complete analytics picture with a single script tag: funnels, journeys, cohorts, session replay, and heatmaps built into one platform, no modules to license.

## Frequently asked questions

Is Umami a good alternative to Piwik PRO?

For most websites, yes. Umami covers events, funnels, journeys, cohorts, segments, session replay, and heatmaps — with a free tier and free self-hosting. Piwik PRO makes sense mainly for enterprises that need its consent manager, CDP, and compliance certifications like HIPAA.

Does Piwik PRO still have a free plan?

No. Piwik PRO discontinued its free Core plan, with existing accounts required to upgrade or migrate by early 2026. Paid plans start around €35/month. Umami Cloud has a free tier, and self-hosting Umami is free forever.

Can I self-host Piwik PRO like Umami?

Only on its top Enterprise tier, via private cloud or on-premises deployment. Umami is open source (MIT) and self-hosts with Docker and PostgreSQL on any server, at no cost.

Does Piwik PRO include session replay and heatmaps?

Not natively — both require third-party integrations. Umami has session replay and heatmaps built in — on Umami Cloud Business plans and free when self-hosting.
