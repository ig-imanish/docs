## Privacy without compromise

Google Analytics relies on cookies and collects vast amounts of user data, requiring cookie consent banners and complex compliance configurations. Umami takes a fundamentally different approach — it tracks no personal data, uses no cookies, and is GDPR compliant out of the box. Your visitors stay anonymous, and you still get the insights you need.

## Analytics your whole team can actually use

GA4 is notorious for its steep learning curve and cluttered interface. Finding a simple answer often means navigating through layers of menus and reports. Umami puts your most important metrics front and center on a clean, real-time dashboard. No training needed — your whole team can start using it immediately.

## Lightweight and fast

The Umami tracking script is under 2KB — over 20x smaller than Google Analytics. That means faster page loads, better Core Web Vitals, and a better experience for your visitors. Every millisecond counts for SEO and conversions.

## Own your data

With Google Analytics, your data lives on Google's servers and is used to power Google's advertising ecosystem. With Umami, you can self-host on your own infrastructure and retain complete ownership of your data. Or use Umami Cloud and rest assured that your data is never shared or sold.

## Frequently asked questions

Is Umami a good alternative to Google Analytics?

Yes. Umami covers the core web analytics you actually use in GA4 — pageviews, referrers, UTM campaigns, custom events, funnels, and revenue — plus session replay and heatmaps, which GA4 does not offer. Because Umami is cookie-free and collects no personal data, you also get GDPR compliance without a consent banner.

Do I need a cookie consent banner with Umami?

No. Umami does not use cookies and does not collect personal data, so it is GDPR and CCPA compliant by default. Google Analytics uses cookies and typically requires a consent banner for visitors in the EU.

How hard is it to switch from Google Analytics to Umami?

Switching takes minutes: add a single ~2KB script tag to your site and data starts flowing in real time. There is no complex property setup, no data sampling, and no waiting for reports to process.

Is Umami really free?

Yes. Umami is open source under the MIT license, so self-hosting is completely free. Umami Cloud also has a free tier, with paid plans starting at $20/month.
