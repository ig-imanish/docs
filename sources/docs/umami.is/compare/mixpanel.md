## The whole journey, not just in-app events

Mixpanel is a powerful product analytics tool designed for tracking in-app user behavior across web and mobile applications. Umami covers the complete journey in one platform — where visitors come from, which campaigns convert, how users move through funnels, and what drives revenue — in a clear, real-time dashboard your whole team can use without an analytics specialist.

## No SDK integration required

Mixpanel requires integrating an SDK into your application and manually instrumenting every event you want to track. Umami works with a single script tag — add one line of code and you're immediately collecting pageviews, referrers, devices, and locations. Custom events are just as easy, requiring only a data attribute on any HTML element.

## Privacy-first approach

Mixpanel tracks individual users with unique identifiers and builds detailed user profiles. Umami takes the opposite approach — it collects no personal data, uses no cookies, and never identifies individual users. You get aggregate insights about your website traffic while fully respecting visitor privacy.

## Accurate data without identity headaches

Mixpanel's identity resolution and user merging can create data accuracy issues — duplicate profiles, inconsistent event counts, and confusing segmentation results. Umami's aggregate approach sidesteps these problems entirely. You get clean, reliable analytics without the complexity of identity management or the risk of misleading data.

## Pricing that grows with you, not against you

Mixpanel's event-based pricing punishes growth — the more successful your product, the higher your bill climbs. Users report that free plan limits change without notice and that costs become difficult to predict. Umami offers straightforward pricing starting with a free tier, and self-hosting the open-source platform is always free. Your analytics bill shouldn't be a tax on success.

## Frequently asked questions

Is Umami a good alternative to Mixpanel?

Yes. Umami covers events, funnels, cohorts, retention, segments, and revenue tracking — the core of what most teams use Mixpanel for — plus the acquisition side Mixpanel is weak on: traffic, referrers, and campaigns. All with a single script tag instead of an SDK integration, and without collecting personal data.

Does Umami have funnels, cohorts, and retention like Mixpanel?

Yes. Umami includes funnel analysis, cohort analysis, retention, segments, and user journeys on every plan. Session replay and heatmaps are part of the platform too — on Umami Cloud Business plans and free when self-hosting.

How does Umami pricing compare to Mixpanel?

Mixpanel uses usage-based event pricing with paid add-ons for session replay and heatmaps. Umami Cloud has a free tier with paid plans from $20/month, with funnels, retention, and revenue reports on every plan. Self-hosting Umami is completely free.

Can I self-host Umami like I can’t with Mixpanel?

Yes. Umami is open source (MIT) and self-hosts with Docker and PostgreSQL, so your data never leaves your infrastructure. Mixpanel is cloud-only — your data lives on their servers.
