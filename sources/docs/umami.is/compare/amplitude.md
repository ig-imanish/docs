## Powerful analytics, not a second job

Amplitude is a powerful product analytics platform, but that power comes at a cost: complexity. Users report that the interface has become increasingly difficult to navigate, with features buried behind layers of menus and settings. Umami covers the full journey — traffic, campaigns, behavior, funnels, retention, and revenue — in a clean dashboard that anyone on your team can understand in minutes, not weeks.

## Pricing that doesn’t punish growth

Amplitude's pricing escalates quickly as your event volume grows, and many of its most useful features are locked behind expensive tiers. Users frequently report that costs become unpredictable and that basic capabilities require an enterprise plan. Umami offers transparent pricing with funnels, retention, and revenue reports on every plan — even the free tier — and self-hosting the open-source platform is always free. Your analytics costs shouldn't spike just because your product is succeeding.

## Built for your whole team

Amplitude is designed for product and engineering teams with deep technical knowledge. Marketing, content, and business teams often struggle with its steep learning curve and complex event taxonomy. Umami is accessible to everyone — add a single script tag, and your entire team gets clear, real-time insights without specialized training or dedicated analytics engineers.

## Reliable data you can trust

As analytics platforms add more features, data reliability can suffer. Users report issues with delayed real-time data, inconsistent historical records, and complex identity resolution that creates confusing results. Umami takes a straightforward approach — aggregate, privacy-first analytics that are accurate and available in real time, without the complexity of user stitching or identity merging.

## Frequently asked questions

Is Umami a good alternative to Amplitude?

Yes. Umami covers funnels, cohorts, retention, segments, revenue tracking, session replay, and heatmaps — plus the acquisition side: traffic, referrers, and campaigns — without Amplitude’s enterprise complexity, custom pricing, or weeks-long learning curve.

Do I need engineers to set up Umami?

No. Umami is a single ~2KB script tag — no SDK integration, no event taxonomy planning, no implementation project. Most teams are collecting data within minutes, and the dashboard is accessible to non-technical users.

Does Umami have cohorts and retention analysis?

Yes. Umami includes cohort analysis, retention, segments, funnels, and user journeys on every plan. Session replay and heatmaps are part of the platform too — on Umami Cloud Business plans and free when self-hosting.

How does Umami pricing compare to Amplitude?

Amplitude uses escalating event-based pricing with premium tiers for advanced features. Umami Cloud is free to start with paid plans from $20/month, with funnels, retention, and revenue reports on every plan — and self-hosting is completely free.
