## Privacy-first, without stopping at pageviews

Umami and Fathom share the same philosophy: cookie-free, privacy-first analytics that don't need a consent banner. The difference is depth. Fathom deliberately stays a single simple dashboard — no funnels, cohorts, segments, session replay, or heatmaps. Umami keeps the simplicity but adds the tools you reach for when a number needs explaining: watch a session, build a funnel, compare cohorts.

## Actively developed open source

Fathom's open-source version, Fathom Lite, has been feature-frozen for years — it receives only security fixes and lags far behind the paid product. Umami's MIT-licensed codebase is the product: the same software behind Umami Cloud, actively developed in the open with hundreds of contributors, and completely free to self-host.

## Free to start

Fathom has no free plan — pricing starts at $15/month after a short trial. Umami Cloud has a free tier so you can start without a credit card, and self-hosting is free forever. When you outgrow the free tier, plans start at $20/month.

## Frequently asked questions

Is Umami a good alternative to Fathom Analytics?

Yes. Both are cookie-free, privacy-first analytics tools, but Umami adds funnels, cohorts, segments, session replay, heatmaps, and custom dashboards that Fathom does not offer — while remaining just as simple to set up.

Is Fathom open source like Umami?

Not really. Fathom Lite, the open-source version, has been feature-frozen since around 2020 and lags far behind the paid product. Umami is fully open source under the MIT license — the self-hosted version is the same actively developed software as Umami Cloud.

Does Fathom have a free plan?

No. Fathom starts at $15/month after a trial. Umami Cloud has a free tier, and self-hosting Umami is completely free.

Does Fathom have funnels or session replay?

No. Fathom focuses on a single simple dashboard and does not offer funnels, cohorts, segments, session replay, or heatmaps. Umami offers all of these — funnels, cohorts, and segments on every plan, with replay and heatmaps on Cloud Business plans and free when self-hosting.
