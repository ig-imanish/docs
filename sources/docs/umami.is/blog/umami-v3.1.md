# Umami v3.1

March 18th, 2026Posted by Mike Cao

![Umami v3.1](https://umami.is/images/blog/image-v3.1.webp)

Today we are excited to announce the release of Umami v3.1 to Umami Cloud.

With v3, we laid the foundation for a faster, more streamlined analytics experience. In v3.1, we are building on that work with features that help teams customize how they share data, understand site performance, and get closer to user behavior.

## New features

### Boards

Boards are now available in Umami.

Boards let you build custom dashboards using the metrics and reports that matter most to you and your team. Instead of sending people to a single all-purpose view, you can create focused dashboards for marketing performance, product engagement, executive reporting, or client updates.

This makes it much easier to organize analytics around a specific goal and share the right information with the right audience.

![image](https://umami.is/images/blog/screenshot-boards.webp)

### White-labeling

We are also introducing white-labeling.

For agencies, consultants, and teams sharing analytics externally, white-labeling makes it easier to present Umami as part of your own product or service. You can provide a more polished experience for clients and stakeholders while keeping the same analytics workflow underneath.

This has been one of the most requested features from users who manage analytics across multiple brands and customer accounts.

### Performance metrics

Umami v3.1 adds performance metrics so you can track page load times and core web vitals directly alongside your analytics data.

Understanding traffic is only part of the picture. Performance has a direct impact on engagement, conversions, and retention, so bringing these metrics into Umami makes it easier to connect site speed with business outcomes.

Now you can spot slow pages, identify trends over time, and use the same reporting workflow you already rely on to understand how performance affects the visitor experience.

![image](https://umami.is/images/blog/screenshot-performance.webp)

### Expanded sharing

Sharing in Umami is no longer limited to websites. You can now share pixels, links, and boards as well.

This gives you more flexibility in how you distribute analytics across your team and with external stakeholders. Whether you want to share a tracking pixel's performance, a link report, or an entire custom board, you can generate a shareable URL the same way you would for a website.

### Multi-value filtering

Filtering has been upgraded to support multiple values for the same field.

Previously, you could only filter by a single value at a time. Now you can filter against several values at once, for example, viewing data where the country is United States, France, or Germany in a single view. This makes it much faster to compare segments and drill into the data without switching between filters.

### Session replays

Another major addition in v3.1 is session replay.

Session replays let you watch real user sessions to better understand how people move through your site, where they get stuck, and what happened before they dropped off. This is especially useful when investigating broken flows, confusing interactions, or pages that are underperforming.

Combined with funnels, journeys, events, and attribution, session replays give you a much clearer view of not just what users did, but why they may have done it.

![image](https://umami.is/images/blog/screenshot-replays.webp)

## New Business plan

We are also introducing a new Business plan for teams that need more scale, collaboration, and advanced capabilities out of the box.

The Business plan includes 10 million events per month, unlimited websites, unlimited team members, 5 year data retention, session replays, white-labeling, Streaming API access, and email and chat support.

This gives growing teams a clearer path between self-serve plans and enterprise, especially if they need higher event volumes and premium features without jumping straight to a custom contract.

## A more complete analytics platform

Umami started as a simple and privacy-first alternative to traditional web analytics tools. Over time, it has grown into a broader platform for understanding traffic, behavior, marketing performance, and now the user experience itself.

With Boards, expanded sharing, multi-value filtering, performance metrics, white-labeling, session replays, and the new Business plan, v3.1 is an important step in that direction. We want Umami to remain simple to use while giving teams the tools they need to go deeper when it matters.

## Thank you

As always, thank you to everyone using Umami, contributing code, reporting issues, and sharing feedback. Your input continues to shape the roadmap and helps us decide what to build next.
