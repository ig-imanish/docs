# Platform

Everything you need to understand your website — traffic, campaigns, behavior, conversions, and revenue — in one privacy-first platform.

Acquisition

## Where your traffic comes from

Understand which channels, campaigns, and content bring people to your website, in real time.

Traffic sources

See where your traffic is coming from to better understand where you should be spending your effort.

UTM tracking

Measure the effectiveness of your campaign by analyzing UTM query parameters that are automatically collected.

Links

Create trackable short links to measure click performance across campaigns.

Pixels

Embed a tracking pixel in emails, ads, or anywhere you can't add JavaScript.

Page views

See which pages get the most traffic, sorted by views, visitors, or time spent.

Visitors

Get detailed information about your visitors like their device, browser, OS and location.

Location

Find out where your visitors are coming from including the city, region and country.

Devices

See the most popular devices used by visitors to help you optimize your pages.

Languages

Know which languages are the most popular among your visitors to help you tailor your content.

Realtime data

See new visits and events the moment they happen — no waiting for batch processing.

Compare

See your metric performance compared against previous date ranges.

Behavior

## What people do once they arrive

Follow the path visitors take through your site, see where they get stuck, and watch it happen for yourself.

Journey

Understand how users navigate through your website.

Session replays

Watch real user sessions to see exactly how visitors interact with your site.

Heatmaps

See click and scroll heatmaps for your pages to understand where visitors focus their attention.

Bounce rate

See which pages keep visitors engaged and which ones they leave immediately.

Segments

Save commonly used filters, so you can quickly reapply them without setting criteria each time

Cohorts

Group your users based on specific actions, helping you uncover trends and measure engagement more effectively

Retention

Measure your website stickiness by tracking how often users return.

Filters

Slice your data by country, browser, URL, or any dimension to find what matters.

Breakdown

Split any metric by dimension to see what's driving your numbers.

Performance

Track page load times and core web vitals to keep your site fast for every visitor.

Privacy

## Privacy by default. No cookies, no personal data.

You get the complete picture with privacy-preserving analytics by default — no cookies, no automatic collection of personal data, and no data sold to advertisers.

GDPR & CCPA

Umami does not automatically collect personal information from your visitors, supporting GDPR and CCPA compliance out of the box.

Data anonymization

Visitor data is anonymized by default to protect your visitors' privacy.

No cookies

Umami does not use any cookies so no annoying cookie banner is required.

Data ownership

Data is always in your control with Umami. You can self-host on your own infrastructure or export your data from Umami Cloud.
