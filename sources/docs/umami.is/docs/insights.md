# Insights

Copy page

_Available since v2.5.0_

Umami has built-in insights to help you gain deeper understanding of your data. Together, these insights offer a comprehensive understanding of user behavior, satisfaction, and opportunities for optimization.

## Available insights[#](https://docs.umami.is/docs/insights#available-insights)

- [Compare](https://docs.umami.is/docs/compare) - See how your website performs versus the past by a variety of metrics.
- [Breakdown](https://docs.umami.is/docs/breakdown) - Dive deeper into your data by using segments and filters.
- [Funnel](https://docs.umami.is/docs/funnel) - Understand the conversion and drop-off rate of users.
- [Retention](https://docs.umami.is/docs/retention) - Measure your website's stickiness by tracking how often users return.
- [UTM](https://docs.umami.is/docs/utm) - Track your campaigns through UTM parameters.
- [Goals](https://docs.umami.is/docs/goals) - Track your goals for pageviews and events.
- [Journey](https://docs.umami.is/docs/journey) - Understand how users navigate through your website.
- [Revenue](https://docs.umami.is/docs/revenue) - Look into your revenue data and how users are spending.
- [Attribution](https://docs.umami.is/docs/attribution) - See how users engage with your marketing and what drives conversions.
